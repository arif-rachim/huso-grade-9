## How to use one file (about 25 minutes per subtopic)
1. **Read** the material (about 12 minutes): start with the opening story, look at the pictures, read the core ideas, study the examples.
2. **Close it**, take a sheet of paper and **redraw from memory** (5 minutes) using the checklist in the ✏️ section. In Earth Science the **cross sections and cycles** are the most useful drawings.
3. **Check your understanding** (5 minutes): 5 questions without opening the material. **You are READY if you get at least 4 out of 5.**
4. **Log what you got wrong** in your Error Log (3 minutes): the topic, why you got it wrong, and a sketch of the correct way.
5. Watch the video or try the simulation in the 🎥 section if you are still stuck. [Stellarium Web](https://stellarium-web.org/) is great for the sky.

> Subtopics that are not READY go on the **red list** and are repeated on the remedial days (9–13 Nov).

## Earth Science habits that win points
- **Read the picture first:** axes, legend, arrows, which side is up, which layer is on top.
- **Units and scale:** AU, parsecs, light-years, km, °C vs. K. Convert before calculating.
- **Process of elimination:** most questions have one or two answers that break a basic rule. Cross them out first.
- **Cause → effect chains:** write them as arrows (warm air rises → low pressure → air flows in).
- Blank answers are 0 points and wrong answers cost 0.25: guess only when you can remove at least one option.
