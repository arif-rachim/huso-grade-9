## 📄 Page 1: Atmosphere, hydrosphere, geosphere

### 🌫️ Atmosphere
| Layer | Height | Temperature | Remember |
|---|---|---|---|
| Troposphere | 0–12 km | falls about 6.5 °C/km | weather, 75 % of the air |
| Stratosphere | 12–50 km | rises | ozone layer |
| Mesosphere | 50–85 km | falls | meteors burn up |
| Thermosphere | 85–600 km | rises | aurora, ISS |
- Composition: N₂ 78 %, O₂ 21 %, Ar 0.9 %, CO₂ about 0.04 %.
- Lapse rates: dry adiabatic **10 °C/km**, wet adiabatic about 5 °C/km.
- **Fronts:** cold (steep, fast: showers, thunderstorms), warm (gentle: long steady rain), stationary, occluded. **High** pressure: sinking air, clear. **Low**: rising air, clouds. Winds: Coriolis turns right in the N hemisphere, left in the S.
- **Circulation cells:** Hadley (0–30°), Ferrel (30–60°), Polar (60–90°). Trade winds from the east, westerlies from the west. ITCZ = rising air, rain.
- **Relative humidity** = vapor ÷ capacity. Dew point = temperature where RH = 100 %.

### 💧 Hydrosphere
- **Discharge Q = A × v** (area × velocity, m³/s).
- Ocean: salinity about 35 ‰; surface currents follow the winds and gyres; deep (overturning) circulation follows density (cold, salty = dense). **Spring tide** = new and full Moon, **neap** = quarter Moons. Waves slow down and grow taller in shallow water. **El Niño:** warm water in the east Pacific.
- **Glaciers** form when snow stays and turns to ice; **erosional**: U-valley, cirque, arête; **depositional**: moraine, drumlin, esker.
- **Groundwater:** aquifer (stores and yields water), water table, porosity (holes) vs. permeability (connected holes). Clay: high porosity but low permeability.

### 🪨 Rocks and minerals
- **Mohs scale:** 1 talc · 2 gypsum · 3 calcite · 4 fluorite · 5 apatite · 6 orthoclase · 7 quartz · 8 topaz · 9 corundum · 10 diamond. Fingernail 2.5, copper coin 3.5, knife 5.5, file 6.5.
- **Rock cycle:** magma → (cooling) igneous → (weathering, erosion, lithification) sedimentary → (heat, pressure) metamorphic → (melting) magma.
| Magma | SiO₂ | Intrusive / extrusive | Color |
|---|---|---|---|
| Felsic | > 65 % | granite / rhyolite | light |
| Intermediate | 55–65 % | diorite / andesite | gray |
| Mafic | 45–55 % | gabbro / basalt | dark |
| Ultramafic | < 45 % | peridotite | green |
- **Bowen:** olivine → pyroxene → amphibole → biotite; Ca-plagioclase → Na-plagioclase; then K-feldspar, muscovite, **quartz last**.
- **Metamorphic grade of shale:** slate → phyllite → schist → gneiss. Limestone → marble. Sandstone → quartzite.
- **Bodies:** dike cuts across layers, sill between them, laccolith dome, batholith > 100 km².
- **Volcanoes:** shield (runny basalt), strato (sticky, explosive), cinder cone.

### 🌍 Interior and time
| Layer | Depth | State |
|---|---|---|
| Crust | 0–35 km | solid |
| Mantle | to 2890 km | solid, flows |
| Outer core | to 5150 km | **liquid** Fe–Ni |
| Inner core | to 6371 km | solid Fe–Ni |
- **P waves:** push-pull, fastest, solids and liquids. **S waves:** shear, solids only. Shadow zone 103°–142°. Distance = Δt(S−P) × v_P v_S / (v_P − v_S).
- **Boundaries:** divergent (ridge), convergent (subduction, collision), transform. **Faults:** normal (hanging wall down, tension), reverse (up, compression), strike-slip (shear).
- **Dating:** superposition, cross-cutting, inclusions. Unconformities: angular, disconformity, nonconformity. **Radiometric:** fraction left = (½)ⁿ, age = n × half-life. C-14 5,730 yr. Earth: **4.54 Gyr**.
- **Maps:** strike and dip tick points down-dip; on flat ground beds get younger down-dip. **Anticline** = oldest in the center; **syncline** = youngest in the center.

## 📄 Page 2: Astronomy

### 🪐 Mechanics and orbits
- **Kepler:** (1) ellipse, Sun at a focus; (2) equal areas in equal times (fastest at perihelion); (3) **T² = a³** (years, AU). Perihelion a(1 − e), aphelion a(1 + e).
- **Gravity:** F = GMm/r². Circular orbit v = √(GM/r). Escape v = √(2GM/r). Binary: m₁r₁ = m₂r₂; **M₁ + M₂ = a³/T²** (Suns, AU, years).
- **Doppler:** Δλ/λ₀ = v/c. Redshift = receding, blueshift = approaching. c = 3.0 × 10⁵ km/s.

### 🔥 Radiation
| Law | Formula |
|---|---|
| Wien | λmax = 2.898 × 10⁶ nm·K ÷ T |
| Stefan–Boltzmann | F = σT⁴, σ = 5.670 × 10⁻⁸ W/m²/K⁴ |
| Luminosity | L = 4πR²σT⁴ |
| Flux | F = L / (4πd²) |
| Magnitude | m₁ − m₂ = −2.5 log(F₁/F₂): 5 mag = ×100 |
| Distance modulus | m − M = 5 log(d / 10 pc) |
| Albedo | absorbed = S(1 − A); T = [S(1 − A)/(4σ)]^¼; Earth A = 0.30, 255 K |
- Parallax: **d (pc) = 1 / p (arcsec)**; 1 pc = 3.26 ly = 206,265 AU; 1 AU = 1.496 × 10⁸ km.

### 🌌 Celestial sphere
- **RA** (hours, 1 h = 15°) and **Dec** (±90°). Altitude of the pole = latitude. Upper culmination h = 90° − |φ − δ|. **Circumpolar if δ > 90° − φ** (north).
- **Sun's declination:** ~20 Mar 0°, ~21 Jun +23.4°, ~22 Sep 0°, ~21 Dec −23.4°.
| Star | Constellation | Note |

| Rigel | β Ori | blue-white supergiant |
| Vega | α Lyr | 25 ly |

| Polaris | α UMi | near the north pole |
- **Deep sky:** M42 emission nebula (Orion) · M31 spiral galaxy (Andromeda) · M1 supernova remnant (Taurus) · M45 open cluster (Taurus).
- **Constellations:** Orion (Ori), Ursa Major (UMa), Ursa Minor (UMi), Cassiopeia (Cas), Lyra (Lyr), Andromeda (And). **Zodiac:** Ari, Tau, Gem, Cnc, Leo, Vir, Lib, Sco, Sgr, Cap, Aqr, Psc.

### ☀️ Solar System
- Order: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars | asteroid belt | Jupiter, Saturn (gas giants), Uranus, Neptune (ice giants) | Kuiper belt (30–50 AU) | Oort cloud.
- **Planet** (IAU): orbits the Sun, round, **cleared its orbit**. Pluto is a dwarf planet.
- **Moon:** sidereal 27.32 d, **synodic 29.53 d**; tidally locked; orbit tilted 5.1°. New: solar eclipse possible. Full: lunar eclipse possible. Eclipses need the Moon near a node.
- **Synodic period:** 1/S = |1/P₁ − 1/P₂|. Mars 780 d, Venus 584 d.
- **Missions:** Sputnik 1 (1957), Gagarin (1961), Apollo 11 (1969), Voyager (1977), Hubble (1990), Cassini (Saturn), Curiosity (2012), New Horizons (Pluto 2015), Chandrayaan-3 (2023).
