## How to use one file (about 25 minutes per subtopic)
1. **Read** the material (about 12 minutes): start with the opening story, look at the pictures, read the core ideas, study the examples.
2. **Close it**, take a sheet of paper, and **redraw from memory** (5 minutes) using the checklist in the ✏️ section. In physics, the **free body diagram** and the **graphs** are the most important drawings.
3. **Check your understanding** (5 minutes): 5 questions without opening the material. **You are READY if you get at least 4 out of 5.**
4. **Log what you got wrong** in your Error Log (3 minutes): the topic, why you got it wrong, and a sketch of the correct way. Always write the **units**.
5. Watch the video or try the simulation in the 🎥 section if you are still stuck. The PhET simulations are great for a visual learner.

> Subtopics that are not READY go on the **red list** and are repeated on the remedial days (9–13 Nov).

## Physics habits that win points
- **Draw first:** a sketch or free body diagram before any formula.
- **Units:** convert to SI (meters, kilograms, seconds, kelvin) before calculating, and check that your answer's unit makes sense.
- **Sense check:** is the answer bigger or smaller than it should be? Does the sign (direction) make sense?
- Use **g = 9.8 m/s²** unless the question says otherwise.
